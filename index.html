§ 02Bill of materials

Nothing deleted, everything archived. Every part that went into the build, in the order it was installed.

PartYearsComponentNotes
  1. Part 012019–2021

    Founder, stint I — the software factory

    Monquick · Co-founder & CCO

    Started a software factory in my third semester of university — two curricula, the one they gave me and the one I chose. Design and development for pharma, agritech, retail and fintech; bootstrapped to 12+ people; products used by 5M+.

  2. Part 022021–2024

    The product builder

    LARVOL · UX lead → Product

    I came to oncology for a personal reason: to understand what was happening to my grandmother. I stayed. Designed the original interface of CLIN, LARVOL’s “Wikipedia for oncology”, and went on to conceptualize and build most of the products LARVOL sells today.

  3. Part 032022–2024

    Founder, stint II — the exit

    RXXO.io · Co-founder & CEO

    Raised $1M+, coined the “Cryptangible” category, launched in Dubai, built a diamond-generation engine that minted 1B assets, and lifted engagement 140% with a UX overhaul. Sold in 2024.

  4. Part 042023

    The hard lesson

    AI startup, Germany · COO

    Moved to Hamburg to build a privacy-first AI startup and hired a team of 40. Then I found the accounts: €305 where €2.5M was supposed to be. I paid 25 salaries out of my own pocket, gathered the evidence, and we sued together — a €4M+ fraud. He went to prison. I came out knowing exactly how little money is worth next to integrity. Read the whole story ↗

  5. Part 052025–2026

    Chief of staff, in all but title

    E3 Group · Early employee

    Built the AI-first pipelines that ran the operation: task flow that kept everyone on time, automated reporting, code review and the occasional front-end. I was the first version of every agent we shipped, and helped win clients with voice agents and identity-verification workflows for truck drivers.

  6. Part 062025–now

    Into capital

    Raíz & Co. · Managing Partner

    Joined to sharpen our advisory work and step into investing — putting capital to work in Latin America, and making it work better.

  7. Part 072025–now

    Selling what I built

    LARVOL · Head of Sales

    Now I lead sales for the products I conceptualized and built — the same person who designed them is the one who sells them.
